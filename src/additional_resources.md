# Additional resources

## Miscellaneous links

- [DBIC website](https://dartmouth.edu/dbic)
- [DBIC on GitHub](https://github.com/dbic)

## MRI collection/analysis

- [DartBrains](https://dartbrains.org) -- the Psyc60 (Dartmouth fMRI Analysis) course by [Prof. Luke Chang (COSANLab)](http://cosanlab.com), with an accent on Python

- [DataLad Handbook](http://handbook.datalad.org) -- all there is to know about using DataLad

- [The Princeton Handbook for Reproducible Neuroimaging](https://brainhack-princeton.github.io/handbook/) -- another great handbook.
  Many questions that are not covered here may well be answered in that resource.
  They also use ReproIn as the naming convention at the scanner.

## con/catenate

The Center for Open Neuroscience maintains [con/catenate](https://github.com/con/catenate), a collection of resources that may be useful to you.
